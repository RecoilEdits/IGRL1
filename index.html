IGRCC RULES

IronGrip Racing Code of Conduct

The Iron Grip Racing League (IGRL) is built on clean racing, strategy, and respectful competition. All drivers must follow these rules to ensure fair, safe, and competitive racing.

LEAGUE RACE SETTINGS

  • Race Length: 50%
  • Tire Wear: 2X
  • Fuel Consumption: 2X
  • Yellow Flags: Relaxed
  • Stages: ON
  • Black Flags: ON
  • Custom Setups: Allowed

CAR NUMBER SYSTEM

  • Drivers claim an active NASCAR number.
  • Manufacturer must match the real-life team.

Manufacturers:

  • Hendrick/Trackhouse → Chevrolet
  • Penske/RFK → Ford
  • Gibbs/23XI → Toyota

DRIVING STANDARDS

Must Do:

  • Hold a predictable line
  • Avoid divebombs
  • Respect space

Not Allowed:

  • Intentional wrecking
  • Brake checking
  • Multi-lane blocking

Lap Car Rules:

  • Yield to faster cars
  • Do not block leaders
  • Be predictable

PENALTIES

  • Pass-through penalty
  • Position loss / Points deduction
  • Race suspension / League removal

CHAMPIONSHIP

  • Traditional points system
  • No playoff format
  • Consistency wins the title
