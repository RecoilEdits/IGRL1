Official Rules & Regulations | IronGrip Racing Code of Conduct

The Iron Grip Racing League (IGRL) is built on clean racing, strategy, and respectful competition. All drivers must follow these rules to ensure fair, safe, and competitive racing.

RACE SETTINGS

  • Race Length: 50%
  • Tire Wear: 2X
  • Fuel Consumption: 2X
  • Yellow Flags: Relaxed
  • Stages: ON
  • Black Flags: ON
  • Custom Setups: Allowed

CAR & TEAM SYSTEM

Drivers claim an active NASCAR number from Cup, Xfinity, Truck, or ARCA. Manufacturer must match the real-life team.

Hendrick Motorsports Chevrolet
Team Penske Ford
Joe Gibbs Racing Toyota

• Number must match claimed car

• Manufacturer must match team

• Custom designs allowed (realism optional)

DRIVING STANDARDS

Must Do:

  • Predictable racing line
  • Avoid aggressive divebombs
  • Avoid reactive blocking
  • Stay aware of surrounding cars
  • Respect other drivers' space

Not Allowed:

  • Intentional wrecking / Retaliation
  • Brake checking
  • Multi-lane blocking
  • Unsafe contact with lap cars

LAP CAR RULES

  • Yield to faster cars and leaders
  • Do not block leaders or winners
  • Maintain predictable lines
  • No sudden moves in front of lead cars
  • Courteous behavior is expected

PROCEDURES

  • Starts & Restarts:
  • No passing before start/finish line
  • Lane discipline on initial start
  • No lane jumping T1/T2 on Lap 1
  • Pit Road:
  • Observe pit speed limits
  • Enter and exit safely
  • Blend properly into traffic
  • Unsafe pit behavior = Penalties
  • Black flags are enforced
