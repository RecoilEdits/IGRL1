IGRCC RULES

IronGrip Racing Code of Conduct

The Iron Grip Racing League (IGRL) is built on clean racing, strategy, and respectful competition. All drivers must follow these rules to ensure fair, safe, and competitive racing.

LEAGUE RACE SETTINGS

  • Race Length: 50%
  • Tire Wear: 2X
  • Fuel Consumption: 2X
  • Yellow Flags: Relaxed
  • Stages: ON
  • Black Flags: ON
  • Custom Setups: Allowed

CAR NUMBER & TEAM SYSTEM

  • Drivers claim an active NASCAR number from Cup, Xfinity, Truck, or ARCA.
  • Manufacturer must match the real-life team.

Teams & Manufacturers:

  • Hendrick Motorsports → Chevrolet
  • Team Penske → Ford
  • Joe Gibbs Racing → Toyota

Paint Schemes:

  • Number must match claimed car
  • Manufacturer must match team
  • Custom designs allowed (realism optional)

DRIVING STANDARDS

Must Do:

  • Hold a predictable racing line
  • Avoid aggressive divebombs or reactive blocking
  • Stay aware of surrounding cars
  • Respect other drivers' space

Not Allowed:

  • Intentional wrecking or retaliation
  • Brake checking
  • Multi-lane blocking
  • Unsafe contact with lap cars

Lap Car Rules:

  • Yield to faster cars and leaders
  • Do not block leaders or race winners
  • Maintain predictable lines
  • Avoid sudden moves in front of lead cars
  • Courteous behavior is expected

STARTS & RESTARTS

  • No passing before the start/finish line
  • Maintain lane discipline on initial start
  • Do not jump lanes through Turns 1 and 2 on Lap 1

PIT ROAD RULES

  • Observe pit speed limits
  • Enter and exit safely
  • Blend properly into traffic
  • Unsafe pit behavior will result in penalties
  • Black flags are enforced

CAUTIONS & INCIDENTS

  • Intentional cautions are prohibited
  • Avoid unnecessary contact; maintain control
  • Admins review incidents; not all contact results in penalties
  • Drivers are responsible for situational awareness

PENALTIES

  • Pass-through penalty
  • Position loss
  • Points deduction
  • Race suspension
  • League removal for severe or repeated offenses
Egregious or repeated violations may result in permanent league bans

COMMUNICATION & CONDUCT

  • No yelling, arguing, or toxic behavior
  • Voice chat must remain race-focused
  • Stay in the main voice channel until qualifying ends
  • After qualifying, drivers may move to separate channels before the race
  • Harassment, intentional distraction, or toxic behavior is prohibited

CHAMPIONSHIP FORMAT

  • Traditional season points system
  • Driver with most points at season end wins the championship
  • No playoff format
  • Consistency and clean racing are rewarded

ADDITIONAL NOTES

  • Admin authority is final on all incidents, penalties, or disputes
  • Cheating, exploiting game mechanics, or unsportsmanlike behavior is prohibited
  • Rules may be updated; drivers will be notified of changes
