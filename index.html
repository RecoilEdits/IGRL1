Official Rules & Regulations | IronGrip Racing Code of Conduct

The Iron Grip Racing League (IGRL) is built on clean racing, strategy, and respectful competition. All drivers must follow these rules to ensure fair, safe, and competitive racing.

RACE SETTINGS

  • Race Length: 50%
  • Tire Wear: 2X
  • Fuel Consumption: 2X
  • Yellow Flags: Relaxed
  • Stages: ON
  • Black Flags: ON
  • Custom Setups: Allowed

NUMBER & TEAMS

Claim active NASCAR numbers from Cup, Xfinity, Truck, or ARCA. Manufacturer must match real-life team.

Hendrick → Chevrolet Penske → Ford Joe Gibbs → Toyota
• Scheme Number must match car
• Custom designs allowed (realism optional)

DRIVING STANDARDS

  • Predictable racing line
  • Aware of surroundings
  • Respect other drivers' space
  • No Intentional wrecking
  • No Brake checking
  • No Multi-lane blocking
  • No Unsafe contact w/ lap cars

PROCEDURES

  • No passing before S/F line
  • Lane discipline on start
  • No lane jumping T1/T2 Lap 1
  • Observe pit speed limits
  • Blend properly into traffic
  • Unsafe pit behavior = Penalties

INCIDENTS

  • Yield to faster cars/leaders
  • Do not block leaders
  • Avoid sudden moves in front
  • Intentional cautions banned
  • Admin reviews all incidents
  • Drivers responsible for awareness

COMMS & STANDINGS

  • No yelling or toxic behavior
  • Stay in main VC until Qualy ends
  • Race-focused voice chat only
  • No Playoff Format
  • Most points wins championship
  • Consistency is rewarded

PENALTY SCALE

Pass-Through Position Loss Points Deduction Race Suspension Permanent Ban

Egregious or repeated violations may result in permanent league bans. Admin authority is final on all incidents, penalties, or disputes.

ADDITIONAL NOTES

  • • Cheating, exploiting game mechanics, or unsportsmanlike behavior is prohibited.
  • • Rules may be updated; drivers will be notified of changes.
  • • Harassment or intentional distraction is strictly prohibited.
